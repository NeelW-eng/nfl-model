Reading the numbers

Units are the amount risked. A 3u bet at −400 risks 3 units to win 0.75, so one loss erases four wins. Edge is the expected profit per unit at FanDuel's price: positive means FanDuel pays more than the win chance deserves, negative means less. The tracker shows over time whether the percentages hold up and whether the picks make money.
