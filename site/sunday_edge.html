How picks are made

Win chance

For every standard Underdog line, nine sportsbooks' over/under prices for the same player and stat are de-vigged and moved to Underdog's number. That consensus is blended 60/40 with the projection model, which uses usage, efficiency, matchup, game script and injuries. Underdog's standard picks all pay the same, so picks are ranked by win chance alone. Reduced-payout lines are skipped.
